Live Metrics, Pay Estimates & Error Jump Banner
  • Each pay period updates RDO authorization vs. usage, SAD leave accrual/ending balance, estimated SAD pay, and 30-day / 60-day OCONUS deadlines automatically.
  • If a policy rule is violated, click the red banner at the top to jump straight to the first error.
Key Policy & Leave Rules Built Into v1
  • Leave Accrual & 49-Day Cap: 8+ qualifying days = +1.25d/PP; 5–7 qualifying days = +0.75d/PP. Capped at 49.00 days effective 03 DEC 2026 (resumes below 47.00d).
  • Special Holiday Passes (02 OCT 2026 Memo): Each holiday Special Pass may be used once, on or after the holiday date, within the 3-pay-period window, and cannot be paired as RDO + RDO or Shift + RDO on weekend holidays.
  • In-Transit (01 APR 2026 Change 2): Bridges On Shift and TDY; cannot sit directly adjacent to RDO, SAD Leave, Terminal Leave, or Special Pass days.
  • REFRAD Report Date: Automatically projects remaining leave as Terminal Leave or post-REFRAD off days.
Exporting for Your Chain of Command
  • Fill out Service Member, Assignment Role, AO / Facility, and Next in Chain of Command in ⚙️ Edit Settings first.
  • Use 🖨️ Export / Print DA-6 PDF for a landscape report (up to 5 pay periods per sheet) or 💾 Download CoC Report to save a standalone HTML copy.
