📖 How to Use the Planner

Quick steps for setting up, planning leave and REFRAD, and saving your schedule.

✓ Your saved plan was upgraded. Review your settings and leave balance before continuing.
1. Set up your planner
  • Open ⚙️ Profile & Settings. Enter your 18 Nov 2026 leave balance; this field is required. Enter 0 if you have no leave balance.
  • Choose your beginning pay period and shift schedule. Add your pay profile and ZIP code if you want an estimated pay calculation.
  • Tap a date in Phone view or click a date in Desktop view to change its status. Open Tools for leave, TDY/travel, REFRAD, and schedule actions.
Status key
  • SHFT: Present / On Shift. RDO: Regular Day Off. LV: State Active Duty (SAD) Leave.
  • TDY: Drill / TDY. TRV: In-Transit travel. SP: Special Pass. INCP: INCAP Pay.
  • RFRD, TERM, and OFF are managed by the REFRAD planner; use Plan REFRAD to change that plan.
Important
  • Your plan saves in this browser on this device. It does not automatically sync between your phone and computer.
  • This is an unofficial planning aid. Verify policy, pay, leave, and approval decisions through your chain of command and official channels.
Plan a day or a date range
  • For one day, select the date and choose SAD Leave in the status picker.
  • For several days, open Tools and choose Plan Leave or Plan TDY / Travel. Select the start and end dates; both dates are included. The planner adds pay periods when needed.
  • For a multi-day TDY plan, answer whether your assigned facility is more than 120 miles from your home station. If it is, the first and last dates are In-Transit with TDY in between; the range must be at least 3 days. If not, every date is TDY. Choosing In-Transit for one date opens a confirmation; choosing No leaves that date unchanged.
Save the SAD Leave Request Form
  • The first time you choose SAD Leave, the planner asks whether you want to save the request form.
  • You can also choose 📄 Download SAD Leave Request Form (PDF) below the schedule at any time.
  • Leave submission deadlines shown in the planner are estimates. Confirm the current requirements with your chain of command.
Choose the date method that matches your plan
  • Open Tools → Plan REFRAD. The planner supports two voluntary date methods; it does not have a separate involuntary REFRAD flow or determine eligibility or approval. Follow your chain of command's instructions for an involuntary REFRAD.
  • REFRAD Report Date: Choose the day you report for work. That day is marked RFRD and counts as Present / On Shift. Terminal Leave starts the next day; Off Mission follows the Terminal Leave block.
  • Mission End Date: Choose the last mission-paid day. Terminal Leave is planned before that date; Off Mission follows it.
  • Review the schedule after applying the plan. It can replace later planned statuses and shorten the displayed schedule after the plan cutoff.
Change or clear a REFRAD plan
  • Open Plan REFRAD to apply a different date or method.
  • To remove the plan, open Tools → Clear REFRAD plan. This restores the schedule that was saved before the plan was applied.
Save the leave form
  • On supported phones and tablets, tap Download SAD Leave Request Form (PDF) and choose Save to Files or another destination in the share menu.
  • On desktop, the button downloads the PDF through your browser. Look in the browser's Downloads location or use its save prompt.
Export your schedule
  • For a printable schedule, choose 🖨️ Print DA Form 6, then select a printer or Save as PDF in the print dialog.
  • To download an HTML copy, choose 💾 Download Duty Plan Report. Add the service member and chain of command details in ⚙️ Profile & Settings first.
Before sharing or submitting
  • Review the dates, leave balance, statuses, and pay assumptions. Estimates and policy checks are not official determinations or approvals.
  • Choose Manage saved data only when you intend to clear the information saved on this device.
